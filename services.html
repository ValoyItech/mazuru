What We Do

Our Services

Expertise

Crafted With Precision

From concept to completion, we offer a full spectrum of interior design and furniture services tailored to transform your space into a masterpiece of elegance and functionality.

Residential Design

Transform your home into a sanctuary of style and comfort. We create bespoke living spaces that reflect your personality while balancing beauty with everyday functionality.

  • Full home interior design
  • Room-by-room styling
  • Colour & texture consultation
  • Furniture layout planning
  • Lighting design & ambiance

Corporate Interiors

Elevate your workspace with professional interior solutions that inspire productivity and reflect your brand identity. From offices to hospitality venues, we design with purpose.

  • Office space planning
  • Brand-aligned interiors
  • Reception & lobby design
  • Boardroom & meeting spaces
  • Hospitality & retail fit-outs

Bespoke Furniture

Custom-crafted furniture designed and built to your exact specifications. Each piece is a unique work of art, combining premium materials with exceptional craftsmanship.

  • Custom sofas & seating
  • Handcrafted dining tables
  • Built-in cabinetry
  • Bed frames & headboards
  • Outdoor & patio furniture

Décor Consulting

Expert guidance on every decorative element of your space. From colour palettes to accessories, we curate cohesive environments that tell your unique story.

  • Colour scheme development
  • Art & accessory curation
  • Fabric & material selection
  • Window treatment design
  • Styling & finishing touches

Project Management

End-to-end oversight of your interior project from initial concept through final installation. We coordinate every detail so you can enjoy a seamless experience.

  • Budget planning & control
  • Contractor coordination
  • Timeline management
  • Quality assurance checks
  • Final installation & handover

Restoration & Upholstery

Breathe new life into cherished furniture pieces. Our skilled craftsmen restore and reupholster with precision, preserving heritage while updating style.

  • Antique furniture restoration
  • Reupholstery services
  • Wood refinishing
  • Frame repair & reinforcement
  • Custom fabric selection

Renovations

Transform existing spaces with our comprehensive renovation services. From structural updates to finishing touches, we breathe new life into homes and commercial properties with precision and style.

  • Home renovations & remodelling
  • Commercial space upgrades
  • Kitchen & bathroom renovations
  • Flooring & ceiling upgrades
  • Structural modifications
